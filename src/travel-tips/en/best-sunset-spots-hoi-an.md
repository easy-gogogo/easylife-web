One of the most common questions at our reception: *"Where do we watch the sunset?"* Here's our honest answer — and one important tip first.

## First: the beach is for sunrise

An Bang and the other beaches around Hội An face east. The sun rises over the sea — that's beautiful, but in the evening the sun sets behind you, over the land. So for sunset, go inland: to the river or the rice fields.

## 1. On the river, from a SUP board

Our favourite, and definitely the best sunset experience in town. You paddle on calm water, the sky turns orange and pink, and the fishermen get their nets ready for the night.

- **Start around 16:30** to be on the water at the right time.
- No experience needed — stand-up paddleboarding is for every age.
- Meeting point is EasyLife Villa, just around the corner from the river.

## 2. In the rice fields, by bicycle

In the late afternoon the light over the rice fields and the vegetable village is soft and golden. Take a bicycle, ride slowly along the small paths and stop wherever you like. From our houses it's only about 10 minutes.

→ [Bicycle rental]({{ 'bicycles' | link }}) — free for EasyLife Villa guests

## 3. Along the riverside in the old town

Stay in the old town after sunset: when the lanterns come on and the boats light up the river, Hội An shows its most famous side. The ride back to Cẩm An takes about 20 minutes by bicycle or 10 minutes by scooter.
