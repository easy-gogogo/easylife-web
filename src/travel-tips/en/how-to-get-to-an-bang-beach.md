An Bang is the beach most of our guests fall in love with: soft sand, palm trees, a row of relaxed beach restaurants and the East Sea right in front of you. Here's how to get there.

## From Nalani Hostel: just walk

Nalani Hostel is a **4-minute walk** from the beach. Flip-flops on, towel under your arm — done. (Towels are free at the hostel.)

## By bicycle: the nicest way

From the old town, the ride to An Bang takes about **20 minutes**. The best part is the route through the rice fields and the vegetable village — quiet paths, green fields and hardly any traffic.

Guests of EasyLife Villa get a bicycle for free. Everyone else can [rent a bicycle from us]({{ 'bicycles' | link }}).

## By scooter: fast and flexible

With a scooter you're at the beach in about **10 minutes** from the old town, and you can easily continue along the coast. We rent automatic scooters right at the hostel and the villa — see [scooter rental]({{ 'motorbikes' | link }}).

## By taxi or Grab

Ride-hailing apps like Grab work well in Hội An. It's the easiest option if you're carrying a lot or coming back late in the evening.

## Tips for your beach day

- **Parking:** many beach restaurants let you park and use their sun loungers if you order food or drinks — just ask before you sit down.
- **Sunrise, not sunset:** An Bang faces east, so it's perfect for sunrise. For sunset, head to the river — or watch it from a [stand-up paddleboard]({{ 'sup' | link }}).
- **Sun protection:** the midday sun is strong, even when it's cloudy. Mornings and late afternoons are the most pleasant.

Want the beach on your doorstep? Stay at [Nalani Hostel]({{ 'hostel' | link }}) — 4 minutes on foot.
